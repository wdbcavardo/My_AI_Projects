# Prompt 02: Ituro ang taste mo (brand sheet)

Pumili ng tatlo hanggang limang video na gusto mo ang style. Pag-aaralan ni Claude ang mga ito at gagawa siya ng **BRAND-SHEET.md** na susundin sa lahat ng edit mo.

**Paano:** palitan ang mga nasa `[BRACKETS]`, tapos i-paste nang buo.

```text
I want my videos to have my own consistent style. Here are 3–5 videos whose style I like:

[LINK 1]
[LINK 2]
[LINK 3]
[LINK 4 — optional]
[LINK 5 — optional]

1. Download them with yt-dlp into "References" (only for studying, not for reuse).
2. Study each one: extract frames, transcribe the audio, and measure the pacing.
   Look at: hook (first 3 seconds), structure, cut rhythm, caption style (font, size,
   position, highlight), colors, layout, motion and transitions, sound design and music
   energy, and the call to action.
3. Find the pattern they share and write me a BRAND-SHEET.md with:
   - my colors (hex), fonts, caption style, layout rules and safe zones for 9:16
   - pacing rules (how often something new appears on screen)
   - motion and transition rules
   - sound rules (music energy, sound effects, voice level)
   - a hook formula and a CTA formula
   - 5 do's and 5 don'ts

The most important rule: make it INSPIRED by these references, not a copy. Never reuse
their footage, music, voice, characters, logos or exact wording. Combine the patterns into
something original that fits me: [DESCRIBE YOURSELF IN 1–2 LINES: your niche,
audience, and the feeling you want].

Show me the brand sheet and wait for my feedback before using it.
```

**Ang pinaka-importanteng linya:** inspired, hindi kopya. Ito ang dahilan kung bakit hindi kamukha ng edits ng iba ang sa iyo.
