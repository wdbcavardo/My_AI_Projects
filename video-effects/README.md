# 17 Video Effects na kayang gawin ni Claude

Kung galing ka sa comment na **LIST**, nasa tamang lugar ka. 👋
Narito ang buong listahan ng 17 effect sa video, kung ano ang itsura ng bawat isa, at isang prompt na puwede mong i-paste kay Claude (o Claude Code).

> **Paalala:** Gumamit lang ng sarili mong footage, o stock video na may malinaw na license. Ang mga effect na may ✂️ ay nangangailangan ng "cutout" (tinatanggal ang background sa likod ng tao), halimbawa gamit ang libreng tool na `rembg`. Laging i-review ang resulta bago i-post.

---

## Paano gamitin

1. Ihanda ang video mo (taong nagsasalita sa camera, maliwanag, plain na background kung kaya).
2. Sa Claude Code, sabihin: *"Use my video `<file>.mp4` and make a 1080x1920 reel with this effect:"* tapos i-paste ang prompt ng effect sa ibaba.
3. Pagkatapos ng unang bersyon, humingi ng ilang still frame at i-adjust (bilis, kulay, laki) hanggang bumagay sa style mo.

---

## Ang 17 effect

| # | Effect | Itsura | Prompt na puwedeng i-paste |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Orbit** | Mga app icon na umiikot sa paligid ng video | `Put my video in a rounded vertical card. Make two app icons orbit around it on a dashed elliptical path, passing behind and in front of the card.` |
| 02 | **Couch wall** ✂️ | Pader na may mga nakasabit na frame sa likod ng tao | `Cut me out of the background and place me in front of a cream wall. Pop in colorful picture frames on the wall behind me, one by one.` |
| 03 | **UI world** | Lumiliit ang video papunta sa loob ng chat app | `Shrink my video into an attachment inside a chat app mockup, then type a short prompt in the input box.` |
| 04 | **Frame break** ✂️ | Lumalaki ang tao at lumalabas sa screen ng phone | `Show my video inside a phone frame. Then scale up a cutout of me so my head and shoulders break out of the top of the frame.` |
| 05 | **Digital zoom** | Mabilis na zoom sa mukha na may viewfinder | `Punch-zoom into my face in two steps (1.6x then 2.3x) with white viewfinder corner brackets and a zoom readout.` |
| 06 | **Phone mock-up** | Video sa loob ng umiikot na phone, parang Reel | `Place my video inside a 3D phone that rotates into view, with Reels-style like/comment/share icons on the right.` |
| 07 | **Checklist** | Glass na checklist na isa-isang nache-check | `Overlay a frosted-glass checklist card on my video with 3 steps that get checked one by one.` |
| 08 | **Headline wall** ✂️ | Mga papel na headline na naka-pin sa pader sa likod ko | `Cut me out and place me in front of a cream wall with pinned paper notes (red pushpins) showing short headlines that pop in.` |
| 09 | **Zoom-through cut** | Lumalapit nang husto, puting flash, malaking "CUT" | `Zoom hard into my video with blur until it flashes white, then show a big bold "CUT" title.` |
| 10 | **Chart grow** | Views counter at lumalaking linya ng chart | `Overlay a stat card on my video: a views counter climbing from 100 to 1.1M with a line chart drawing upward.` |
| 11 | **Pull-back reveal** | Lumalayo ang camera at nasa loob pala ng editing app | `Pull back from full-screen to reveal my video inside a video editor window with a timeline and a rendering progress bar.` |
| 12 | **Green screen** ✂️ | Napapalitan ang background ng iba't ibang kulay | `Cut me out and swap my background through bold colors (green, purple, orange).` |
| 13 | **Paper cut** ✂️ | Parang ginupit na papel na may puting outline | `Cut me out with a thick white outline and drop shadow, like a paper cutout on a paper-textured background.` |
| 14 | **Doodle boil** ✂️ | Black & white na may kumikislot na doodle | `Make me black and white with a white outline, and add hand-drawn doodles (crown, heart, star, arrow) that wiggle every few frames.` |
| 15 | **Vertigo** ✂️ | Dolly zoom: pareho ang laki ko, gumagalaw ang likod | `Keep my cutout the same size while the background with perspective lines zooms in, like a dolly zoom.` |
| 16 | **Cover story** ✂️ | Magazine cover na may malaking title sa likod ng ulo | `Turn the frame into a magazine cover: big red serif title behind my head, issue number, barcode, and a "NO EDITOR NEEDED" tag.` |
| 17 | **Glass type** ✂️ | Malaking salitang parang salamin sa makulay na background | `Put me in front of a purple-pink gradient and add large frosted-glass outline text over the bottom of the frame.` |

---

**Tip:** Isang effect lang muna bawat eksena, mga 1 hanggang 3 segundo. Mas engaging kapag sabay sa salitang binibigkas mo.
